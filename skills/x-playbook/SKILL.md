# x-playbook

Turn the author's material into an X **thread**: a hook post, a body of single-idea posts, and a payoff. Most readers see only the hook in their feed; the rest exists only for those who tap it.

Use only facts, numbers and stories the author gave. Never invent a stat to make a hook stronger.

## 1. Find the payoff first

Write one sentence: what the reader has when they finish (a method, a number, a story's lesson). Then list the 4-8 points that get them there, one idea each. Done when the payoff line and the point list exist.

## 2. Body

- One idea per post. Its first line is a mini-hook that stands alone.
- Post 2 states the stakes: why the reader should care, before the details.
- Concrete over general: real numbers ("10,400 users", not "many"), names, before/after.
- Short lines, white space, no walls of text. Stay under the 280-character limit per post unless the author has a premium account and asks for long posts.
- 5-12 posts total. Cut any post that repeats a point.

## 3. Hook (write it last)

The hook post must do three things: stop the scroll, promise the payoff, and open a loop the body closes. Patterns:

- A specific result plus how: "I cut our AWS bill from $41k to $9k in 6 weeks. Here's every change:"
- A claim against common belief, with proof to follow.
- A story's first moment, cut at the tension.

Write 3 hooks and pick the one with the most concrete number or image. Leave out "Thread:", "A thread 🧵 on..." and other labels that announce effort. Done when the pick names the payoff and holds a specific detail.

## 4. Payoff and close

- Last body post delivers the payoff in one line the reader could quote.
- Closing post: a short recap, then one ask (follow for more on X, reply with your case, the link). Put any outside link here or in a reply, not in the hook.

## Output

Numbered posts (1/, 2/, ...), the 2 rejected hooks, and the payoff line. Write in English.
