# content-diagnosis: English

Researched from English sources, borrow tier (see Sources). Not a translation of the Chinese method.

## Read metrics as a funnel

Each metric is one step: saw it, clicked it, stayed, finished, acted. The drop between two neighbouring steps points at the part of the piece in between. Mailchimp's reading of email: good opens with weak clicks is "a content or design problem rather than a list quality issue". Nielsen Norman Group: "The first 10 seconds of the page visit are critical"; visitors who stay past that often stay minutes.

## Signal table

| Signal | Likely causes, most likely first | Look at | Change next time |
|---|---|---|---|
| High opens or clicks to the post, few shares, replies or likes | Weak ending; the title promised more than the body paid | last three paragraphs; title against body | End on the story's turning point and the lesson in one line; close with a question readers can answer |
| Low open or click rate, but those who read share it | Title, subject line or preview fails | title, subject line, preview text, social card | Rewrite the title with a number, a conflict or the reader's own phrase; reshare with the new title |
| High bounce, time on page under ~15 seconds | Slow opening; value not clear in the first screen | first screen | Put the conflict, result or number in the first two sentences |
| Readers leave midway (scroll depth stalls) | Middle sprawls; no signposts | middle section | Add subheads; one point per section; cut the weakest section |
| Email: opens fine, clicks low | Content or call to action unclear | the call to action, layout | One clear call to action, said in plain words, placed early and repeated at the end |
| Many likes, few follows or subscribes | Reader does not know what more they would get | author bio, ending, other recent posts | Say what the next pieces cover; keep the topic focused |
| HN / Reddit: upvoted early, then sinks; or comments argue with the premise | Title overclaimed; one weak fact in the post | title, the disputed claim | State the claim at the strength the evidence supports; answer the top comment in an update |
| Many saves or bookmarks, few shares | Useful but nothing a reader wants to be seen agreeing with | the opinion line | Add one clear opinion or quotable line |
| Unsubscribes or unfollows spike after the piece | Off-topic for the audience, or frequency jumped | topic against past pieces | Return to the core topic; flag off-topic pieces up front |

## Baselines

Use the author's own median first. Platform benchmarks move every year; when one is needed, quote it with its source and date (for email, Mailchimp's benchmarks page).
