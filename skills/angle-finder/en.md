# angle-finder: English

Researched from English sources, borrow tier (see Sources). Not a translation of the Chinese method.

## The bar: new to the reader

Paul Graham's test for useful writing: it tells people something true and important "they didn't already know", stated as strongly as it can be without becoming false. An angle passes when a reader of the default post would still learn something from it.

## Read comments for emotion (voice-of-customer mining)

Copywriters call this review mining or message mining: read what people already say about the topic, copy their exact phrases and emotions, and let those set the message.

- Where: top HN comments on past posts about the topic (search hn.algolia.com), top Reddit comments in the topic's subreddit, 2-4 star Amazon reviews of books on the topic, app store reviews, niche forums.
- Read the mid-rated and most-upvoted ones: people explain what they wanted and what was missing.
- Keep the exact phrase. A phrase readers already use makes a headline they recognize.

## Role switch (the Rashomon move)

Tell the same event from a different party. Fiction calls it Rashomon style: each witness tells a different, partial version.

1. List every party: the user, the payer, the on-call engineer, the person who left, the customer who complained, the tool itself.
2. Strike the party every post speaks for (usually the builder or the expert).
3. Write the angle as that party's version of events, in the emotion the comments show.

Example (made up): topic "we moved to microservices". Default: architecture lessons. Switch to the on-call engineer: "Our diagram got nicer and my pager got 4x louder".

## Forced combination

James Webb Young: "an idea is nothing more or less than a new combination of old elements"; the skill is seeing relationships between facts.

1. Take the topic's key noun.
2. Pair it with an opposite, or a concept from an unrelated field (finance, sports, cooking, medicine).
3. Keep the pair only if the author's material explains why it holds.

Example (made up): "schema migrations" + "surgery checklists": "Your migration tool should ask before it cuts".

## Writing the angle

- One sentence, headline-ready, with a concrete noun or number.
- English default angles to avoid: "N lessons from X", "X is dead", "Why I switched to X", a tutorial when the material is a story.
