# story-424: English story parts and rules

Researched from English sources (listed at the end), not translated from the Chinese book. The 4+3+2 shape stays so the output labels match across languages; each part below is filled with the English practice that does the same job.

## 4 steps: the skeleton

| Part | What to write |
|---|---|
| One line | The story in one sentence, start and result. Test it with the story spine: "Once upon a time... Every day... One day... Because of that... Until finally..." If the spine has no "One day", there is no story yet. |
| Conflict | What the person wanted and what stood in the way. The spine's "One day" is the break in routine. |
| Reason | Why the conflict happened: the spine's "Because of that" links, taken from the author's account, not supplied. |
| Combine | Join the line, conflict and reason into one causal chain. Each beat should follow from the last ("because of that"), not just come after it ("and then"). |

## 3 points: the flesh

| Part | What to write |
|---|---|
| Background | The "Every day": the routine before the break. Only what the reader needs to feel the break. |
| Core message | The one thing the reader carries away; a number, decision or line. In an article it is the story's link to the nut graf. |
| Emotional peak | The moment with the most at stake, written as concrete details the author gave (Chekhov: not "the moon is shining" but the glint on broken glass). |

## 2 moves: ignite

| Move | How |
|---|---|
| Rearrange order | Start in medias res: open on the peak or the conflict, then backfill the background. One jump only. |
| Show, don't tell | Replace each emotion word with an action, object or spoken line the author gave. If no such detail exists, keep the plain statement and do not invent one. |

## Platform notes

- Blog or newsletter: the story sits inside an argument; end the passage on the core message's concrete detail, then return to the essay.
- HN: keep the story short (one paragraph or two) and technical: the conflict is the bug, the reason is the cause.
- Short video: the first 3 seconds are the conflict or the peak; background is one caption line.
