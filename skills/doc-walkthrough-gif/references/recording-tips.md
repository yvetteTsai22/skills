# Tips to give the user before a (re-)recording

- Keep the acted-on item's detail page open: its activity log is the reliable evidence of automatic changes. Board views hide cancelled issues and may not live-update.
- Avoid scrolling member/assignee lists (less to blur).
- Widen windows so every relevant column is visible at once, including In Review.
- Keep recording one poll past the last action so the final state lands on screen without a manual reload.
- Stop recording a few seconds after the final state; the macOS end-of-recording dimming gets cut anyway.
- Close or scroll away chats that show colleagues' names.
