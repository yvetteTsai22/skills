# trend-hook: English

Researched from English sources, borrow tier (see Sources). Not a translation of the Chinese method.

## Trend sources and how each ranks

- **Google Trends, Trending Now** (trends.google.com/trending): search queries with a recent surge, refreshed about every 10 minutes, filterable to the past 4h/24h/48h/7d, marked Active or Ended. Use it to confirm a trend is live and to pick the words people search for. An Ended trend can come back.
- **Hacker News front page** (news.ycombinator.com): points divided by a power of time since submission, plus flags and moderation. Tech and "intellectual curiosity" only; politics, crime, sports and celebrity news are off-topic unless they show a new phenomenon. Titles stay plain: no editorializing, no "10 ways to" numbers.
  - Hook here = the author's piece answers the question the front-page thread is arguing about. Read the top comments for it.
- **X trending** (Explore): picked by an algorithm, tailored by default to who you follow, your interests and location, and favors topics popular *now* over steady ones. Switch location to see what a wider audience sees; post count is only one factor.
- **Reddit rising** (reddit.com/r/<sub>/rising): posts gaining upvotes fast (relative to age and views), before they reach Hot. Small subreddits stay on Rising for an hour or more; big ones turn over in minutes. Check the topic's own subreddits, not r/all.

## Judge the stage

- Rising: on Reddit Rising, a new Trending Now entry, a front-page HN post under a few hours old. Best time to hook.
- Peak: X trend with many explainer posts, HN post with hundreds of comments. Hook only with something the threads lack.
- Ended / fading: Google Trends marks it Ended, or it has left the front page. Skip unless the author has new data.
- Scheduled: dates known ahead (product launches, conferences, earnings, holidays, anniversaries). Prepare 1-2 weeks out.

## Hook moves

- **Newsjacking**: add your own view or data to a breaking story while journalists still look for a second paragraph. The window is the rising stage; after the peak, the hook reads as late.
- **Missing piece**: the trend's threads keep asking a question the author's material answers. Example (made up): HN thread on a major cloud outage + author's postmortem → "Our status page said green for 41 minutes. Here's why it couldn't say anything else."
- **Same question, other field**: the trend and the topic share an underlying question; name it in the hook. Use `angle-finder` for the role switch or forced pairing.

## Writing the hook

- One sentence, the trend's searchable term in the first half, plus one concrete noun or number from the author.
- For HN, write the title plain and put the trend link in the first comment.
- Skip tragedies and private misfortune; a hook that profits from them costs more trust than it gains reach.
